      START HERE

      Build the decision habit

      Choose Preflop, Postflop, Facing pressure or Adaptive mix above the table. Read the action history and choose without a lesson prompt giving away the theme. After ten decisions, pause for a set review before starting the next ten. Immediate retries do not advance the set or change your first-attempt score. The feedback shows the cost of your choice, the action mix and a practical takeaway.

      Good decisions include low-frequency alternatives with similar EV. Focus on avoiding costly mistakes before trying to reproduce an exact mix. Use Try again to practise immediately without changing your first-attempt score. Mistakes stay available for a delayed check after at least a day and three other decisions. Delayed checks test recall of a spot; broader mastery still needs unfamiliar boards.

      Preflop uses a PokerData 100bb reference; source convergence and rake are not reported. Postflop uses separate authored range models across contrasting board textures, including river-only overbet and all-in models with declared entering ranges. These are individual drills, not a single connected full-hand solution. Unrestricted replays still have coverage gaps.

      COACHING SCENARIOS

      Explore reads and adjustments

      In Explore’s coaching scenarios, seven in every ten picks target your review themes, due repetitions, or recent mistakes. When both are available, about one third of targeted picks replay your own hands; the rest use coached drills. Three in ten picks keep your general game sharp. Filters narrow both pools.

      Missed concepts return after a few other hands. Successful practice spaces them to 1, 3, 7, then 14 days. Several correct reps across different spots are needed before a concept moves to maintenance.

      04 / PROGRESSION

      Accurate, then fluent

      Start with foundations. Gradual mode opens applied strategy after at least 12 scored decisions with 80% on-line or reasonable in the latest 12. Decision time never changes the grade.

      Suit permutations preserve the poker situation. Different authored hands and boards teach transfer; cosmetic changes alone are not evidence of mastery.

      05 / TABLE READS

      Range, then adjustment

      Dry high-card boards often support small continuation bets. Connected boards that favor the caller call for more checking. Position and the number of opponents matter.

      Against players who call too much, prioritize larger value bets and fewer bluffs. Against a passive player who suddenly raises big, reassess one-pair hands. Use the reads supplied in the drill.

      06 / THE PRICE

      Know your price

      With a pot of P before a bet of B, calling requires B / (P + 2B) equity. A half-pot bet needs 25%; a pot-sized bet needs 33.3%.

      For a simplified heads-up river model: a pure bluff breaks even at B / (P + B) folds. Minimum defense frequency is P / (P + B), a range benchmark, not a command to call with any particular hand.
